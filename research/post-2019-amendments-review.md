# Post-2019 legal amendments search — 2026-11-26

Scope: 15 April 2019 through 26 November 2026; municipal and alternate MiColombiaDigital domain, indexed municipal norms, vLex, local news, SECOP procurement indexes. Searches combined Decreto 090/2019, reestructuración, modificación, derogatoria, abandono, evaluación, rutas 6/12/14, Guafilla and TUYO. Municipal normativity landing pages returned no readable index in this session; this was a targeted indexed search, not an exhaustive act-by-act municipal archive audit.

## Conclusion

No permanent route-specific amendment, revocation, or numerical headway replacement after Decreto 090/2019 was found in this search. Retain all 14 as legal-baseline research candidates, keep the 42 official schedule records unchanged, no claim that all 14 operate. Eight are source-enabled; ninth current route unidentified. No evidence establishes automatic expiration of the three evaluated routes. Legal-status field remains subject to later acts; no service activation or public release change. Use official street descriptions for reconstruction and reconciliation, preserving the Article 9 stopping-site constraint. Before publishing an actual passenger timetable reconcile actual service; do not silently represent authorized-but-unserved routes as running.
