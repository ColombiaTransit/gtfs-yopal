## Legal search vocabulary

National Decreto 1079/2015, art. 2.2.1.1.8.6, distinguishes unjustified service reduction of more than 50% or failure to start within the authorized period from a formal revocation following demonstrated abandonment for 30 consecutive days. Art. 2.2.1.1.8.7 provides for a company to request a declaration of vacancy when unable to serve authorized services. These provisions help target searches; no finding is made that a particular Yopal operator meets the legal test. Source: https://apps.procuraduria.gov.co/gd_1952/docs/d1079015.htm (articles 2.2.1.1.8.6 and .8.7, reviewed). Search also for desistimiento de servicios, declaratoria de vacancia, revocatoria del permiso, reducción de capacidad transportadora and acts resolving appeals or reinstatement.
