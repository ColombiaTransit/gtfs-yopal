## Approved pattern contract
data/approved-patterns.json maps all fourteen string route IDs to objects with shape_approved, stop_policy_verified, reuse_verified (true only after review), source, stop_source, time_source, synthetic=false, shape (ordered [lon,lat] vertices, closed), and stops (ordered records: stop_id, name, lon, lat, shape_index, arrival_secs, departure_secs, designated_stop=true). Stop index must increase; first/last are same verified stop, first departure zero, last index at end. Stops within 50m of indexed geometry and strictly increasing elapsed time. Times should be derived from validated geometry and documented travel-time/dwell model. The generator rejects incomplete route coverage or unverified boarding, and won't invent or approve inputs. Not yet present. Use data/reviewed-anchors/<route_id>.json with route_id, reviewed=true, coordinates (ordered closed list) for GraphHopper candidate construction. No endpoint-only fictional passenger stop model.

## Second increment: reconstruct all circuits

Run 45834451412 succeeded (31,242 OSM objects, 478 legal waypoint candidates: 140 unique intersections, 157 multiple, 181 unresolved; schedule plan and synthetic validator executed, no actual feed). Route 9 full-way relation pilot 22,703.4 m, seven joins 1,062.2 m; not approved. The new batch reconstructs all fourteen ordered legal waypoint chains against directed OSM bus-access topology. Query additionally retrieves named landmarks and turn restrictions within the existing expanded extent. Explicit aliases and 65 m separated-road intersection candidates are labeled; landmark snaps <=120 m are candidates only. Dynamic programming selects a connected sequence using shared named-road weighting and a reversal penalty. It connects missing intermediate waypoints as hypotheses, never silently confirms them; missing origin/end, closure, long legs, repeated edges, reversals and known turn restrictions are reported. Via-node turn restrictions are checked after routing, not enforced; conditional and via-way restrictions remain unsupported. Graph may choose the wrong street when several candidates fit, and aliases may not be exact; review all cases. Partial closed circuit is not a complete verified route, and cannot be used as an approved GTFS pattern. Outputs all-route-candidates.geojson, circuit-summary.csv, circuit-reconstruction-report.json. No change to schedules or boarding model. OSM attribution applies. No general archive work.
