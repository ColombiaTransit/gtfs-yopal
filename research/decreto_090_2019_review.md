## Operative articles
- Article 4 (p. 5): Coocatrans Ltda. routes 2,8,11; Cootrallanero Ltda. 1,3,10; Flota Sugamuxi S.A. 4,5,7; Llano Líneas S.A. 6,9,12. Routes 13 and 14 are jointly operated by all four companies; their company-specific dispatch allocations are determined internally. The act uses variant spellings/legal suffixes for Cootrallanero in different sections; retain Article 4 spelling pending identity verification.
- Articles 5–7 (pp. 5–14): adopt the 14-route service structure and prescribe maximum headways in minutes, start/end operating hours and ordered full-circuit descriptions. All fourteen descriptions return to their initial location. Route 14 starts at La Guafilla. This supports one full circuit per dispatch, rather than duplicating frequency on each branch; the exact last dispatch and intermediate layovers are not explicit.
- Article 8 paragraph 1 (p. 14): routes 6,12,14 subject to a six-month evaluation from issuance. The article does NOT expressly terminate the authorization at six months. Seek evaluation results or later modifying acts; do not assume either automatic expiry or confirmed current operation.
- Article 9 (p. 14): control of stopping at legally established sites referenced in Article 17 of Decreto 267/2018, under national traffic law. This is contrary evidence to unrestricted continuous boarding; do not treat missing stop data as authorization for continuous pickup/drop-off. Reopen the boarding-model decision and seek the referenced maps or current rule.
- Article 14–15 (p. 15): technically justified changes possible; effective upon publication and repeals conflicting earlier provisions. Exact publication date and subsequent amendments not established.
