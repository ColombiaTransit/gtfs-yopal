Article 3 adds title IV chapter I arts.29A–29G, including urban interchange/parking design and future minimum urban capacity of 10% of intermunicipal boarding bays; Article 5 adopts official cartography; Article 6 delegates planning/construction responsibilities. The map depicts a proposed terminal parcel, not bus route shapes or proof of commissioning. Do not move current stops or route termini based on this act.
